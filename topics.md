# Episode Topic Bank

20 ready-to-produce episode ideas with exact sources. All footage listed
is free public domain — no licence fee, no permission needed.

---

## 1. Miracle on the Hudson (US Airways 1549)
**Angle:** The 208 seconds between bird strike and water landing.
**Footage:** NTSB docket AAR-10-03 — cockpit voice recorder transcript,
radar track animation, NTSB press footage.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA09MA026"

---

## 2. United 232 — The Impossible Landing
**Angle:** How three pilots flew a plane with zero hydraulics using only thrust.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-90-06, NASA Ames crash simulation footage (public domain),
original news footage now public domain via archive.org.
**Search:** archive.org → search "United 232 Sioux City"

---

## 3. Gimli Glider — Ran Out of Fuel at 41,000ft
**Angle:** Metric vs imperial unit error that caused Air Canada 143 to glide
to a disused airstrip.
**Footage:** TSB Canada docket — publicly available. NASA glider comparison
footage from images.nasa.gov.
**Search:** bst-tsb.gc.ca → search "Air Canada 143"

---

## 4. Aloha Airlines 243 — Roof Tears Off Mid-Flight
**Angle:** Metal fatigue, pressurisation cycles, and why one flight attendant
did not survive.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-89-03, FAA structural fatigue test footage (public domain).
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA88MA054"

---

## 5. Air France 447 — Disappeared Over the Atlantic
**Angle:** Pitot tube icing, stall at cruise altitude, why the crew never
recovered.
**Footage:** BEA France final report (publicly available), radar reconstruction
animations released by BEA.
**Search:** bea.aero → search "AF447"

---

## 6. TACA 110 — Landed a Jet on a Grass Levee
**Angle:** Both engines flamed out in a thunderstorm. Crew glided a 737 onto
a grass strip with no hydraulics.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-89-04. Archive.org news footage public domain.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA88AA015"

---

## 7. British Airways 9 — All Four Engines Quit Over Indonesia
**Angle:** Volcanic ash killed every engine on a 747. Crew restarted three
while gliding silently over the ocean.
**Footage:** AAIB UK report publicly available, NASA volcanic ash research
footage from images.nasa.gov.
**Search:** assets.publishing.service.gov.uk → search "Boeing 747 Jakarta 1982"

---

## 8. Southwest 1380 — Engine Explosion, Window Blown Out
**Angle:** Uncontained engine failure. One passenger was partially ejected.
Pilot Jennifer Shults saved 143 lives.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-19-02, NTSB animation publicly released.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA18MA142"

---

## 9. TransAsia 235 — Pilot Shut Down the Wrong Engine
**Angle:** Engine failure on takeoff, crew shut down the working engine, aircraft
rolled and hit a bridge.
**Footage:** ASC Taiwan final report + CVR transcript (public). Dashcam footage
widely licensed — ViralHog or AP Archive.
**Search:** asc.gov.tw → search "GE235"

---

## 10. Air Canada 759 — Almost Landed on a Taxiway Full of Planes
**Angle:** Night visual approach, lined up on the wrong surface, four jets
sitting on the taxiway in the dark.
**Footage:** TSB Canada A17F0038, FAA ATC audio (FOIA available).
**Search:** bst-tsb.gc.ca → search "A17F0038"

---

## 11. Delta 191 — Wind Shear Kills a Widebody on Final
**Angle:** Microburst on approach, 133 dead. Changed how every airport
detects wind shear today.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-86-05. NASA wind shear research footage from images.nasa.gov.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DFW 1985 microburst"

---

## 12. LOT Polish 016 — Gear-Up Landing in Warsaw
**Angle:** Complete hydraulic failure. Crew prepared 231 passengers for a
belly landing and executed it perfectly. Zero fatalities.
**Footage:** PKBWL Poland report public. Airport CCTV footage — licensed via
AP Archive or Reuters.
**Search:** pkbwl.gov.pl → search "LOT 016 2011"

---

## 13. Alaska Airlines 261 — Jackscrew Failure
**Angle:** A stripped jackscrew sent a MD-83 into an uncontrolled dive.
Crew flew inverted trying to recover.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-02-01, full CVR transcript public, NTSB animation released.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA00MA017"

---

## 14. US Air Force Thunderbirds — Mid-Air Survival
**Angle:** Formation flying incidents and the ejection systems that saved pilots.
**Footage:** US Air Force public affairs footage — all public domain.
**Search:** dvidshub.net → search "Thunderbirds incident"

---

## 15. Qantas 32 — A380 Engine Explodes Over Singapore
**Angle:** Uncontained engine failure shredded 650 system components. Crew
worked through hundreds of ECAM warnings to land safely.
**Footage:** ATSB AO-2010-089, public photos and CVR summary released.
**Search:** atsb.gov.au → search "QF32"

---

## 16. NASA X-15 — Fastest Plane Ever Flown
**Angle:** Mach 6.7. The test pilots who pushed it and the one who didn't
come back.
**Footage:** NASA Dryden/Armstrong footage — all public domain.
**Search:** images.nasa.gov → search "X-15"

## 17. Air Midwest 5481 — Improperly Rigged Controls
**Angle:** Maintenance error set the elevator trim so the plane was
uncontrollable from the moment it rotated.
**Footage:** NTSB AAR-04-01, CVR transcript public.
**Search:** ntsb.gov → search "DCA03MA022"

---

## 18. Crossair 3597 — Spatial Disorientation at Night
**Angle:** Pilot ignored his instruments. CFIT on approach in darkness.
**Footage:** Swiss BFU report public. NASA spatial disorientation training
footage from images.nasa.gov.
**Search:** bfu.admin.ch → search "Crossair 3597"

## 19. China Airlines 120 — Caught Fire After Landing
**Angle:** Everyone evacuated in 90 seconds before the 737 exploded.
**Footage:** JTSB Japan report public. Airport CCTV — licensed via AP Archive.
**Search:** mlit.go.jp → search "China Airlines 120 2007"

**Search:** dvidshub.net → search "Blue Angels"

## How to use this list

1. Pick a topic
2. Download the investigation report (PDF) — read the CVR transcript
3. Get footage from the source listed (all free)
4. Run `python3 new_episode.py` — fill in the fields
5. `make build EP=episode_00X.json`
